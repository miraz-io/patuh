# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (first public version)

- DPIA screening (UU PDP Article 34(2) and GDPR/EDPB criteria), risk register, controls, dashboard.
- DPIA export as Word in English or Indonesian, with dated sign-off.
- Regulation library with full-text search; optional AI assistant that cites and proposes, never decides.
- Roles (admin, DPO, member, auditor), password and OIDC sign-in, audit log (changes, exports, searches, AI chats).
- Security: email+IP and IP sign-in limits, per-account rate limit, AI key encrypted at rest, guarded AI endpoint address.
- Interactive tutorials (EN/ID), mobile layout, language remembered.
- Docker image, CI. PP 33/2026 is not included (no official text yet).
